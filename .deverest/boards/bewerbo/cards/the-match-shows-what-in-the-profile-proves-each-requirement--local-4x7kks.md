The match lists the requirements it found and reports only that none are covered. The concept shows, for each covered requirement, which position or qualification proves it, and lets a missing certificate be filed on the spot. Without that the user cannot tell whether a gap is in their profile or in the reading of the advert.

**Acceptance criteria**
- A covered requirement names the profile entry that covers it.
- A requirement waiting on a document offers to file that document.
- The count of covered requirements reflects what the profile actually holds.
