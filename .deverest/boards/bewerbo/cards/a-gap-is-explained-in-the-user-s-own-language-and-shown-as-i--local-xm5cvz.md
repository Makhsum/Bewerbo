German readers treat an unexplained break in the chronology as a warning sign, and nearly everyone who moved country has one. The concept lets the user name the reason in their own language and see straight away the sentence that will stand in the Lebenslauf — Umzug, Sprachkurs, Anerkennungsverfahren. Neither the naming nor the preview exists yet.

**Acceptance criteria**
- A gap found in the timeline is offered for explanation in the user's own language.
- The user sees the German wording that will appear in the document before the document is produced.
- An explained gap is no longer counted as outstanding.
