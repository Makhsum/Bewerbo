Bewerbo presents itself as ordinary software: what it does feels like something anything could do, nothing in the app reveals the AI behind it, and entering it involves no sign-in. The effort a user spends does not pay off in the result. Its features and its design need a concept that makes the product's value immediate.

**Requested by the user:** possibly a chat with the AI that then creates everything — and a concept first, before anything is built.

**Acceptance criteria**
- A user doing a minimum of work receives a result that visibly exceeds that effort.
- The AI behind the product is apparent to a user from using the app.
- Entering the app goes through a sign-in, so what a user produces is theirs.
- A concept for the features and the design is decided before it is built.
