A degree earned outside Germany means little to a German recruiter on its own. The concept shows each education entry with how its institution and qualification are classified in anabin, and whether a ZAB assessment is still open. The app records neither, so a foreign qualification reaches the Lebenslauf unframed and the reader has no way to weigh it.

**Acceptance criteria**
- An education entry records how the qualification is classified and whether its assessment is still open.
- That standing appears beside the degree in the produced Lebenslauf.
- An entry still awaiting assessment is shown as outstanding rather than as settled.
