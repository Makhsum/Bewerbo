German readers weigh what an applicant achieved rather than what they were responsible for, and a list of duties translated straight from a foreign CV reads as weak. The concept offers to turn the duties entered under a position into results. The app only stores what was typed.

**Acceptance criteria**
- A user can have the duties of a position rewritten as outcomes.
- The user sees the original and the rewrite and decides which reaches the document.
