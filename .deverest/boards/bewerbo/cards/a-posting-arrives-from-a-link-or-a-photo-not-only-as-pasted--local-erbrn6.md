A posting can only be entered by pasting its text. On a phone the advert is usually behind a link or on the screen of another app, and copying the whole of it by hand is the step at which people give up. The concept offers all three ways in.

**Acceptance criteria**
- A posting can be taken from a link.
- A posting can be taken from a photo or a screenshot.
- Whatever the source, the text that was read is shown and can be corrected before it is used.
