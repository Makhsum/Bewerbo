The review counts how many sentences begin with "Ich" and reports one of three. On a letter of three sentences that proportion carries no meaning, and presenting it as a finding spends the reader's attention on noise.

**Acceptance criteria**
- A check that rests on proportions is shown only once the text is long enough for it to mean something.
- A check held back that way is not presented as having passed.
