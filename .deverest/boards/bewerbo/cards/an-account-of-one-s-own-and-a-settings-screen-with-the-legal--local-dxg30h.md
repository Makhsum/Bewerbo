The app has no account and no settings. A user cannot see what is held about them, change the interface language, read the terms, or have their data deleted — and what the app handles are CVs, which are personal data under the DSGVO. Nothing about the operator is stated anywhere, which German law requires of a published service.

**Requested by the user:** terms of use (AGB) and the legal pages that belong with them.

**Acceptance criteria**
- A user has an account of their own and can reach it from the app.
- Settings hold the interface language and the legal pages — terms, privacy notice and imprint.
- A user can see what is stored about them, export it, and have it deleted.
